# Design Principles & UI/UX Standards

## Core Philosophy: Avoid "Zombie UI"
Stop building generic, soulless interfaces. When designing and building interfaces for Rahat Setu (web portal, admin command center, or mobile application), strictly uphold these non-negotiables:

1. **Refuse to confuse done with good**:
   - Speed and polish do not equal quality.
   - A component is not complete just because it compiles without errors or uses standard boilerplate. It must feel crafted, intentional, and ergonomically sound for real users under pressure (e.g. mega-crowds, emergencies, intense sunlight, noisy venues).

2. **Have a clear POV (Point of View)**:
   - Set human standards so AI doesn't default to generic outputs or standard "grey card on white background" templates.
   - Establish bold, intentional visual hierarchy, purpose-built typography, high-contrast states for outdoor readability, and distinct status signifiers (e.g., critical crowd alert vs informational schedule update).

3. **Encode intent**:
   - Embed design standards directly into components, styles, design tokens, and workflows.
   - Maintain a cohesive design language across the visitor mobile app, visitor web portal, and organizer command center.

4. **Embrace post-build editing**:
   - Human quality control shifts to rigorous editing at the end.
   - Scrutinize every detail: spacing consistency, motion curves, micro-interactions, responsive behavior across mobile viewports, touch targets (>48px for outdoor/crowd use), and low-light / high-glare legibility.

5. **Raise the ceiling**:
   - Use AI to invent new, intuitive interfaces, not just reproduce old ones faster.
   - Think beyond standard lists: interactive heatmaps, glanceable queue wait-meters, spatial audio indicators, tactile emergency SOS flows, and situational context cards that dynamically change based on where the visitor is standing.
